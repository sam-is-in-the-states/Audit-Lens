This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the

1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Crestview Asset Management, LLC, 2425 East Camelback Road, Phoenix, AZ (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
October 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription. A hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”) for up
to thirty (30) authorized users, including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription Term. The
Platform is provided solely as a hosted service.
3.2 Data Migration. RevLens will perform a data migration (the “Implementation”) converting and reconciling
Customer’s historical positions, lots, and transaction history into the Platform and validating the migrated data
against Customer’s books. The migrated data set is a prerequisite to Customer operating its accounts on the
Platform. The Implementation is scoped specifically to Customer’s data, systems, and requirements and is not
offered by RevLens on a standalone basis. Customer’s production access to the Platform will be
provisioned upon Customer’s written acceptance of the completed Implementation.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
Item Amount
Platform Subscription (annual) $15,000
Data Migration (one-time) $4,500
Total $19,500
The Total is payable in advance, in full, within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date. The migration fee is
non-refundable.
RENEWAL
This Order Form renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms at RevLens’s then-current list
pricing unless either party gives notice at least thirty (30) days before term end. The Data Migration is provided
once and is not repeated on renewal.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
