This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the

1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Cedar Falls Investments, LLC, 800 Nicollet Mall, Minneapolis, MN (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
August 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
RevLens grants Customer a hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”),
including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription Term. The subscription includes a base
allotment of up to one thousand five hundred (1,500) processed account transactions per calendar month. The
Platform is provided solely as a hosted service.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
5.1 Base Subscription Fee. $15,000 per year, payable in advance, in full, within thirty (30) days of the Effective
Date.
5.2 Usage Fees. Transactions in excess of the monthly base allotment are billed at $0.04 per transaction,
subject to an aggregate cap of $3,000 in overage charges over the Subscription Term; Customer will not
be charged for overage beyond that cap. RevLens meters usage and invoices overage monthly, in arrears.
Invoices are due net thirty (30) days.
RENEWAL
This Order Form renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms at RevLens’s then-current list
pricing unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before term end.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
