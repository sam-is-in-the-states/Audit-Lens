REVLENS, INC.
SUBSCRIPTION ORDER FORM & SERVICE TERMS
Order Form No. RL-2025-0307
This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Harborview Investment Management, LLC, 60 Harbor Plaza, Stamford, CT (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
March 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription. A hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”) for up
to seventy-five (75) authorized users, including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription
Term. The Platform is provided solely as a hosted service. The subscription includes a base allotment of up to
two thousand (2,000) processed account transactions per calendar month.
3.2 Implementation Services. RevLens will perform implementation services (the “Implementation”) to prepare
the Platform for Customer’s production use, comprising migration of Customer’s historical portfolio and account
data, configuration of Customer’s custodial feeds and book-of-record integration, and build-out of Customer’s
firm-specific compliance rules and reporting templates. The Implementation is scoped specifically to Customer’s
data and operating environment and is not offered by RevLens on a standalone basis. Customer’s production
access to the Platform will be provisioned upon Customer’s written acceptance of the completed
Implementation.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
Item Amount
Platform Subscription (annual) $24,000
Implementation Services (one-time) $6,000
Total fixed fees $30,000
5.1 Fixed Fees. The Total fixed fees of $30,000 are payable in advance, in full, within thirty (30) days of the
Effective Date. The Implementation fee is non-refundable.
5.2 Usage Fees. For each processed account transaction in excess of the monthly base allotment, Customer
will pay an overage fee of $0.06 per transaction. Usage is metered by the Platform; RevLens will calculate
actual metered usage at the end of each calendar month and invoice Customer monthly, in arrears. Overage
invoices are due net thirty (30) days. No credit is given for unused base allotment.
6. RENEWAL
The subscription renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms at RevLens’s then-current list
pricing unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the
then-current term. Implementation Services are provided once and are not repeated on renewal.
7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
