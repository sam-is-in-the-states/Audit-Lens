This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Windsor Financial Partners, LLC, 1700 Pacific Avenue, Dallas, TX (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
April 15, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription. A hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”) for up
to seventy (70) authorized users, including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription Term.
The Platform is provided solely as a hosted service.
3.2 Implementation Services. The Implementation comprises both standard configuration steps (account
provisioning and template loading) and a custom integration of the Platform with Customer’s in-house portfolio
accounting system and proprietary data warehouse. The custom integration is required for the Platform to
operate within Customer’s environment, and the standard and custom elements are delivered as a single
combined engagement. The Implementation is scoped specifically to Customer’s data, systems, and
requirements and is not offered by RevLens on a standalone basis. Customer’s production access to the
Platform will be provisioned upon Customer’s written acceptance of the completed Implementation.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date of April 15, 2025.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
Item Amount
Platform Subscription (annual) $36,000
Implementation Services (one-time) $8,000
Total $44,000
The subscription fee is billed quarterly in advance in installments of $9,000 each. The Implementation fee of
$8,000 is billed on the Effective Date. Invoices are due net thirty (30) days.
RENEWAL
The subscription renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms at RevLens’s then-current list
pricing unless either party gives notice at least thirty (30) days before term end. Implementation Services are
provided once and are not repeated on renewal.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
