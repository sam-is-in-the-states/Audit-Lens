REVLENS, INC.
SUBSCRIPTION ORDER FORM & SERVICE TERMS
Order Form No. RL-2025-0119
This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Meridian Capital Partners, LLC, 88 Battery Street, San Francisco, CA (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
January 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
RevLens grants Customer a hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”),
including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription Term. The Platform is provided solely as
a hosted service.
The subscription includes a base allotment of up to one thousand (1,000) processed account transactions per
calendar month. “Processed account transactions” means trades, rebalances, and reconciliations executed
through the Platform.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
5.1 Base Subscription Fee. $12,000 per year, payable in advance, in full, within thirty (30) days of the Effective
Date.
5.2 Usage Fees. For each processed account transaction in excess of the monthly base allotment, Customer
will pay an overage fee of $0.05 per transaction. Usage is metered by the Platform. RevLens will calculate
actual metered usage at the end of each calendar month and invoice Customer monthly, in arrears, for overage
incurred in that month. Overage invoices are due net thirty (30) days.
5.3. No credit or refund is given for unused base allotment in any month.
6. RENEWAL
This Order Form renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms at RevLens’s then-current list
pricing unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the
then-current term.
7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
