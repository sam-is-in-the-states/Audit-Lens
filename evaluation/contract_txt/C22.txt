This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Tidewater Capital Advisors, LLC, 1450 Brickell Avenue, Miami, FL (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
April 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
RevLens grants Customer a hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”),
including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription Term. The Platform is provided solely as
a hosted service.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
5.1 Minimum Annual Commitment. Customer commits to a minimum annual fee of $24,000 ($2,000 per
month), payable in advance, in full, within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date. The minimum commitment
entitles Customer to process up to two thousand (2,000) account transactions per month and is payable in full
regardless of actual usage.
5.2 Usage Above Commitment. Account transactions processed in excess of the monthly included volume are
billed at $0.10 per transaction. RevLens meters usage and invoices excess monthly, in arrears. Invoices are due
net thirty (30) days.
RENEWAL
This Order Form renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms at RevLens’s then-current list
pricing unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before term end.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
