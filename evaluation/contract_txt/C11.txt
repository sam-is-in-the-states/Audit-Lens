REVLENS, INC.
SUBSCRIPTION ORDER FORM & SERVICE TERMS
Order Form No. RL-2025-0633
This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Cedar Point Wealth Advisors, LP, 1750 Camelback Road, Phoenix, AZ (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
July 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription. RevLens grants Customer a hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management
Platform (the “Platform”) for up to forty (40) authorized users, including standard updates and standard support,
for the Subscription Term. The Platform is provided solely as a hosted service.
3.2 Implementation Services. RevLens will perform implementation services (the “Implementation”) to prepare
the Platform for Customer’s production use. The Implementation includes:
(a) migration and reconciliation of Customer’s historical account and position data from Customer’s
incumbent systems;
(b) configuration of Customer’s custodial data feeds and integration with Customer’s existing book-of-record;
(c) build-out of Customer’s firm-specific compliance rule sets, household groupings, and reporting templates;
and
(d) configuration and validation testing tailored to Customer’s operating environment.
The Implementation is scoped specifically to Customer’s data, systems, and requirements and is not offered by
RevLens on a standalone basis. Customer’s production access to the Platform will be provisioned upon
Customer’s written acceptance of the completed Implementation, expected to occur within approximately
ninety (90) days of the Effective Date.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
Item Amount
Platform Subscription (annual) $12,000
Implementation Services (one-time) $3,000
Total $15,000
The Total is payable in advance, in full, within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date. The Implementation fee is
non-refundable.
6. RENEWAL
This Order Form renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month subscription terms at RevLens’s
then-current list pricing unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the
end of the then-current term. Implementation Services are provided once and are not repeated on renewal.
7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
