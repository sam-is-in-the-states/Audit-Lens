SUBSCRIPTION ORDER FORM & SERVICE TERMS
Order Form No. RL-2025-0173
This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Greenfield Capital Partners, LLC, 150 North Riverside Plaza, Chicago, IL (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
May 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription. A hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”) for up
to fifty-five (55) authorized users, including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription Term.
The Platform is provided solely as a hosted service.
3.2 Onboarding Engagement. RevLens will deliver a defined, one-time onboarding engagement (the
“Onboarding”) following its standard methodology, comprising a standard historical-data conversion and
standard custodial-feed setup, delivered and accepted prior to go-live. The Onboarding has standalone value, is
of a type available from independent implementation firms, and is not required for Customer’s subscription
access.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Thirty-six (36) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
Item Amount
Platform Subscription ($18,000/yr × 3) $54,000
Onboarding Engagement (one-time) $6,000
Total contract value $60,000
The subscription fee of $18,000 per contract year is billed annually in advance, the first year on the Effective
Date and each subsequent year on its anniversary. The Onboarding fee of $6,000 is billed on the Effective Date.
Invoices are due net thirty (30) days.
RENEWAL
Following the thirty-six (36) month term, the subscription renews for successive twelve (12) month terms at
RevLens’s then-current list pricing unless either party gives notice at least thirty (30) days before term end.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
