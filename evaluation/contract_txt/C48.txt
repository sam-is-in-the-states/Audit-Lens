This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the

1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Castlerock Wealth Partners, LLC, 1801 California Street, Denver, CO (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
July 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription & Implementation. A hosted subscription to the Platform together with a customized
implementation tailored to Customer’s environment, on which production access depends. The Implementation

standalone basis. Customer’s production access to the Platform will be provisioned upon Customer’s
written acceptance of the completed Implementation.
3.2 License to RevLens Quantitative Library. RevLens grants Customer a license to use the RevLens
Quantitative Analytics Library, RevLens’s proprietary intellectual property, within the Platform during the term, in
consideration of the royalty in Section 5.3.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twenty-four (24) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
Item Amount
Subscription ($30,000/yr × 2) $60,000
Implementation (one-time) $8,000
Bundle discount ($5,000)
Total fixed fees $63,000

attributed to a specific component. Subscription is billed annually in advance ($30,000/yr); implementation and
the first annual fee are billed on the Effective Date.
5.3 Library Royalty. Customer will pay a royalty of $0.18 for each analytics run executed against the licensed
Library, metered and invoiced monthly in arrears.
RENEWAL
Following the twenty-four (24) month term, the subscription renews for successive twelve (12) month terms at
RevLens’s then-current list pricing unless either party gives notice at least thirty (30) days before term end.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
