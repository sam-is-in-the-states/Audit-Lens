REVLENS, INC.
SUBSCRIPTION ORDER FORM & SERVICE TERMS
Order Form No. RL-2025-0341
This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Beacon Hill Advisors, LLC, 100 Beacon Street, Boston, MA (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
June 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription. A hosted subscription to the RevLens Portfolio Management Platform (the “Platform”) for up
to sixty (60) authorized users, including standard updates and standard support, for the Subscription Term. The
Platform is provided solely as a hosted service.
3.2 Onboarding Engagement. RevLens will deliver a defined, one-time onboarding engagement (the
“Onboarding”) consisting of a standard historical-data conversion and a standard custodial-feed connection,
delivered and accepted by Customer prior to subscription go-live. The Onboarding follows RevLens’s standard
methodology, has value to Customer independent of the ongoing subscription, and is of a type also offered by
independent implementation firms. Customer’s subscription access does not depend on RevLens performing
the Onboarding.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twenty-four (24) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
Item Amount
Platform Subscription ($30,000/yr × 2) $60,000
Onboarding Engagement (one-time) $5,000
Total contract value $65,000
The subscription fee of $30,000 per contract year is billed annually in advance, the first year on the Effective
Date and the second on its anniversary. The Onboarding fee of $5,000 is billed on the Effective Date. Invoices
are due net thirty (30) days.
RENEWAL
Following the twenty-four (24) month term, the subscription renews for successive twelve (12) month terms at
RevLens’s then-current list pricing unless either party gives notice at least thirty (30) days before term end.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
