SUBSCRIPTION ORDER FORM & SERVICE TERMS
Order Form No. RL-2025-0391
This Order Form is entered into under and incorporates the RevLens Master Subscription Agreement (“MSA”) between the
parties.
1. PARTIES
Provider: RevLens, Inc., 200 Congress Street, Boston, MA (“RevLens”).
Customer: Slateharbor Capital, LLC, 1000 Second Avenue, Seattle, WA (“Customer”).
2. EFFECTIVE DATE
November 1, 2025 (the “Effective Date”).
3. SERVICES
3.1 Subscription. A hosted subscription to the Platform, including standard updates and standard support, for
the Subscription Term.
3.2 Implementation Services. RevLens will perform implementation comprising data migration, custodial-feed
configuration, and compliance-rule build-out tailored to Customer. The Implementation is scoped specifically to
Customer’s data, systems, and requirements and is not offered by RevLens on a standalone basis. Customer’s
production access to the Platform will be provisioned upon Customer’s written acceptance of the
completed Implementation.
4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
Twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.
5. FEES & PAYMENT
5.1 Combined Subscription & Implementation. The combined subscription and implementation fee is
$22,000 (comprising a $18,000 subscription and a $4,000 implementation), equating to $1,833 per month, with a
monthly minimum of that amount billed in arrears.
5.2 Usage Fees. Processed account transactions above the included monthly allotment are charged at $0.025
per transaction. For each month, Customer pays the greater of the monthly minimum or actual metered usage.
RevLens invoices monthly, in arrears; monthly usage is expected to fluctuate over the term.
RENEWAL
This Order Form renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms at RevLens’s then-current list
pricing unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before term end.
TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for the other’s uncured material breach on thirty (30) days’ written notice.
